RMRF LABS

the sandboxes — one problem each, isolated from the game

Every one of these exists because something was hard to see inside a running match. They load standalone, take no setup, and are safe to break.

DECISIONS what the commanders are thinking, and why

AI Lab · Decision Spine
ai-lab/index.html
The live console. Watch both commanders side by side as a match runs — the mission scores with their term-by-term maths, the target choice, and the driver state, fed by a cheap publish hook rather than a debugger. Also holds the commander flowcharts (commanders.html, flowchart.html), the behaviour editor.html, and expr.html for reading a nested condition as boxes.
Standoff Playground
standoff-lab.html
Where a sieger should stand. A 2D sandbox for picking a firing position — flood the reachable ground, keep the cells in range with a line to the target and clear of the other guns, take the nearest. The game's solver was rebuilt to match this after the lab kept finding spots the game abandoned.

LOOK visual work, tuned against a still frame instead of a firefight

Grass Playground
grass.html
Blade style, gradient, wind and density, with sliders. The unlit-blade look with a root lighter than the ground was chosen here, and it beat an exact-match root.
Fire Lab
fire.html
Fire for things that blow up — a vehicle, a fuel depot. Yomotsu's volumetric fire (lifted from the shipwrecked campfire, textures regenerated procedurally so no image files ship) against a batched billboard, plus a bursts mode that spawns short-lived fires from a fixed pool, which is the way the game would actually use it. Reports the peak concurrent count, because that — not the total — is what costs. Open it on the real GPU; a software renderer tells you nothing about speed.
Surf Lab
surf.html
The shoreline — wave shape, foam and how the water meets the sand.
