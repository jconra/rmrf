TWO CHANGES, GATED TOGETHER
  1. SHOT-AWARENESS. damageVehicle stored only a TIMESTAMP when an enemy vehicle hit us
     (_hitByVehT) while a TURRET hit already stored a position (_hitByTurret). Worse, every
     combat trigger in AI.js keys off view.seesEnemy FIRST — `underFire` is only consulted after
     a sighting already passed — so a unit hit from outside its forward sight cone fell through
     the entire ladder to advance/assault and drove on oblivious. Since sight became a cone this
     is common: measured 73% / 15% / 44% of under-fire ticks (seeds 18/25/39) came from an
     attacker the unit could not see.
     Fix: record _hitByVeh {x,z,t}; stamp _lastEnemyPos (shot:true) so the commander learns the
     position; new `ambushed` trigger -> pursue toward the shot origin, below engage/suppress/
     retreat and above the mission goal. Intel only — seesEnemy stays false, so the unit must
     SEE the attacker before it can fire back. A/B knob: cfg.shotAware.
  2. noReach — THE DANCING LURCHERS. Reproduced on Jacob's seed 1851068760. When the driver
     refused a `pursue` goal the issuer cleared v.ai.lastSeen — futile, because if the unit could
     still SEE the target perception rewrote lastSeen the next tick, pursue re-triggered, the
     driver refused again. That branch RETURNS before firing or moving, so the unit burned every
     tick writing off the same contact. Measured 20 write-offs/sec = one per tick.
     Fix: record v.ai.noReach {x,z,t} at the refusal; `pursuing` declines the same contact for
     12s within 20u.
