WHAT CHANGED (js/AI.js only)

    flee/retreat instead of being forced to stand.
  - kite consults fightScore: a positive score never retreats. The 0.55 hull cliff became a
    gradual urge (cfg.kitePivot / kiteMin) and the distance trigger gained hysteresis while
    engaged — it was a hard step with none, which is what made the pair pump.
  - kite falls back at most cfg.kiteFallback (34u) instead of to the home base.
  - `combat` never destructured cfg — the new knobs threw a ReferenceError until the probe hit it.

THE REGRESSION, STATED PLAINLY
  transit-stuck by state gains a `flee 57` class that did not exist before, and near-water stuck
  rose 189 -> 250. That is the direct consequence of the change: units that were previously
  PINNED in a lost fight now disengage, and some of them wedge while fleeing, particularly near
  shorelines. The behaviour is correct and the wedging is a separate (pre-existing) nav weakness
  now being exercised more often. Knobs to walk it back if needed: cfg.fofBail (raise toward 0 =
  fewer units bail), cfg.kiteMin, cfg.kiteFallback.
